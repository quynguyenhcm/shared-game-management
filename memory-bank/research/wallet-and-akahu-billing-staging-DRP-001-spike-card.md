### TRAP Candidates
- Using enduring `POST /payments` (user token + connected account) or "pay-anyone" consent: removed/limited by official open banking, forces per-player token storage and per-team re-consent. Flagged as a wrong-product trap.
- Copying the enduring webhook verification (`X-Akahu-Signature`, SDK `validateWebhook`) onto the one-off payments webhook: headers, key endpoint and host differ (`X-Akahu-Payments-*`, `api.payments.akahu.io`). Flagged.
- Treating `FAILED/REJECTED` as "destination account bad" and auto-setting `teams.payout_blocked_at`: `REJECTED` also covers Akahu fraud-check and generic bank rejection, so a payer-side rejection could wrongly block a team's payouts. Flagged.
- Treating `FAILED/BANK_ERROR_INDETERMINATE` as safe to retry: docs say it is unclear whether the payment will be processed. Double-payment risk. Flagged.
- Assuming webhooks carry a unique event id or amount/payer detail: they do not; relying on payload alone loses idempotency and ledger data. Flagged.
- Hardcoding bank limits or assuming enduring-consent limits apply to one-off: limits are served at runtime by `GET /v1/connections` and one-off applicability of the published table is unconfirmed. Flagged.
- Production go-live gated by a sales contract plus Tier 1 accreditation review with no published lead time: Phase 4 schedule risk. Flagged.

### Open Questions
- Third-party payee permission (highest priority, needs Akahu support): under the One-Off Payments contract, may an accredited app pay arbitrary client-supplied payee name + account number (here, many different team accounts the platform does not own)? Is payee verification (e.g. `CLIENT_VERIFIED` liability) required, and does collecting on behalf of teams make us a "facilitator" needing special terms?
- Destination-account rejection signal: which status/reason (or 400 code) does Akahu produce when the payee account is closed/invalid/non-BECS? Is it `invalid_payee` at create, `FAILED/REJECTED`, or something not documented? Without this, `payout_blocked_at` (AC-ERROR-4) needs a different trigger (e.g. manual/ops review).
- Does the bank/Akahu validate payee name against account number (name matching), and what happens on mismatch?
- Auth header format for the payments API: overview says HTTP Basic (app token user, app secret password); OpenAPI says only scheme `AppSecret`. Confirm in sandbox. Also which origin hosts sandbox vs production.
- Sandbox access: is one-off payments enabled on a self-serve developer app, or only after contacting Akahu sales (pricing page implies credentials follow a conversation)? Can a sandbox app point at `demo_bank` for one-off payments without a contract?
- Sandbox gaps: how to simulate user cancel (`USER_CANCELLED`) and destination rejection in Demo Bank; Demo Bank 5.15 is described both as a decline (insufficient funds) and as `REJECTED` on the official connection: confirm exact status/reason produced.
- Webhook semantics: unique event id absent: confirm dedupe approach with Akahu; confirm webhook signing padding (PKCS1 vs PSS) and exact byte handling for the payments webhook; key rotation/lifetime policy and unknown-key-id behaviour; whether the `webhook_uri` can be changed after creation; delivery order guarantees beyond `emitted_at`; whether retries stop after 20 or after a time window.
- Limits: actual one-off per-bank `single_payment`/`daily`/`weekly` values from `GET /v1/connections`; applicability of the published enduring table (ANZ $10,000 downlevel etc.) to one-off; Kiwibank limit; business-account payers (BNZ note says business customers cannot yet use open banking payments at higher limit).
- Production lead time and conditions: contract turnaround, Tier 1 review duration, any minimum volume, whether staging-environment access suffices for review, and whether the consumer-information-page and MFA expectations in the accreditation page apply to a one-off-payments-only app.
- Payer account eligibility: which payer accounts cannot authorise one-off payments (savings, KiwiSaver, joint/multi-signatory, business), and what status results (`MFA_UNSUPPORTED`, `MULTISIG_UNSUPPORTED` exist only in the enduring vocabulary).
- Reversal/refund: Akahu states a payment cannot be reversed once initiated (unverified search snippet). Confirm refund handling expectations for the wallet product (outside Akahu: payer must be refunded from the team's account or wallet credit).
- Idempotency of `POST /v1/one-off-payments`: no idempotency key documented; confirm behaviour on retry after timeout (P5: no Akahu call under a DB lock; domain write + enqueue in one tx).
- Fees: confirm $0.15 per successful request is current and whether it is per initiated or per `SENT` payment, and GST treatment.
- Whether DRP-002 (alternative NZ pay-by-bank providers) is needed: Akahu One-Off Payments appears technically capable (see matrix); this is conditional only on the third-party payee permission question above. Decision belongs to the orchestrator/synthesis.
