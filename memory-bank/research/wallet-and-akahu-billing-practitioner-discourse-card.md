### TRAP Candidates
- T1: Akahu payments are whitelist-only and accreditation may cap payable destination accounts and max amounts; a platform paying many unrelated team accounts might hit a per-app payee restriction (Akahu docs plus accreditation language; approval lead time unknown).
- T2: Payee-verification regime (CLIENT_VERIFIED requires client-accepted liability; NONE needs Akahu enablement) may apply to team-supplied account numbers; could force liability or a verification flow not in the spec.
- T3: `SENT` is not instant credit for all banks (ANZ delay anecdote); also `PAUSED` manual review has no SLA, so "pending" can last long (PayHero reports first-time-payee approvals).
- T4: Webhook has no unique event id and delivery is best-effort ("retries may not cover every failure"); out-of-order possible. A reconciliation poller is mandatory.
- T5: Payer cancelling or abandoning the redirect leaves ambiguous states (PayHero "sent but unpaid"; Akahu `TIMED_OUT` after 1 hr default); wallet/payment state machine must treat expiry as terminal and not double-credit on late authorisation.
- T6: Reference/particulars/code fields capped at under 13 chars of restricted charset; reconciliation by free-text reference is unreliable, must key on Akahu payment `_id`.
- T7: No reversal/chargeback; refunds need a separate outbound mechanism and payee details.
- T8: 35c per-payment fee (PayHero-reported, integrator pricing may differ from Akahu direct) is material on small fees (a few dollars) for a wallet top-up/billing model.
- T9: Docs pages for statuses/limits are enduring-payment pages; whether one-off payments share `PAUSED`/`DECLINED`/`ERROR` and the $100k limit is unconfirmed (docs may be conflated).

### Operational Pitfalls
- New payee often triggers a separate approval step ("Awaiting Approval", emailed by Akahu) before money moves — [PayHero help](https://support.payhero.co.nz/hc/en-us/articles/4613098770831) (Grade C)
- Partial failures: some payments in a batch succeed while others fail; must check per-payment status — [PayHero help](https://support.payhero.co.nz/hc/en-us/articles/4613098770831) (Grade C)
- Payer cancelling at the Akahu step leaves the pay marked sent but unpaid — [PayHero help](https://support.payhero.co.nz/hc/en-us/articles/4613098770831) (Grade C)
- Per-bank daily/per-payment limits (ANZ $25k, ASB $10k, BNZ $50k/day, Westpac $30k/day) — [PayHero help](https://support.payhero.co.nz/hc/en-us/articles/4613098770831) (Grade C)
- 35c fee per payee payment — [PayHero help](https://support.payhero.co.nz/hc/en-us/articles/4613098770831) (Grade C)
- Webhook: no event id, best-effort retries, advised fallback poll, 10s response deadline — [Akahu monitoring docs](https://developers.akahu.nz/docs/oneoff-payments-monitoring-progress) (Grade C, vendor)
- `PAUSED` manual review with no time bound; payments irreversible once initiated — [Akahu Making a Payment](https://developers.akahu.nz/docs/making-a-payment) (Grade C, vendor)
- ANZ-via-Akahu settlement delay of a few hours — [Stacked Bitcoin help](https://support.stackedbitcoin.com/support/getting-started/anz-akahu-payments) (Grade C, snippet only, unverified)
- Reference fields under 13 chars, restricted charset — [Akahu one-off docs](https://developers.akahu.nz/docs/oneoff-payments-making-a-payment) (Grade C, vendor)
